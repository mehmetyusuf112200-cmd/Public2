--- ENGLISH ---
Short description (80):
Clear the parking jam and match passengers to buses! A relaxing color puzzle.

Full description:
The parking lot is jammed and passengers are waiting at the stop! 🚌
In Commute Craze every vehicle points one way. Tap a vehicle with a clear path and it drives to the stop. Passengers of the same color hop on automatically, and full vehicles head off!

🧩 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap vehicles that have a clear way out
• Match passengers to vehicles of their color
• If the stop fills up, you lose – plan your order!
